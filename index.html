3. Performance, Stability & Geometric Constraints (R1–R11)

To maintain C1 differentiability for gradient-based optimization, element-level von Mises stress ratios ae = σvM,e / σallow are aggregated via a Kreisselmeier-Steinhauser (KS) smooth maximum function with aggregation parameter ρ = 60:

KSρ(a) = amax + (1/ρ) ln ∑e exp[ρ(ae − amax)] ≤ 1 (5)
